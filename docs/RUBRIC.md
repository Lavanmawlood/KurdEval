# Annotation Scoring Rubric

Each model response to a Kurdish prompt is scored on a 1–5 scale.

## 5 — Excellent
Fluent, natural Kurdish. Grammatically correct. Reads as if
written by a native speaker. No unnecessary code-switching to
Arabic/Turkish/English.

## 4 — Good
Clear and understandable. Minor grammatical issues or slightly
unnatural phrasing, but does not affect comprehension.

## 3 — Acceptable
Understandable but with noticeable language issues — repeated
words, awkward sentence structure, or moderate code-switching.

## 2 — Poor
Significant problems: severe repetition, broken grammar, heavy
mixing with non-Kurdish vocabulary where Kurdish equivalents
exist. Meaning is hard to follow.

## 1 — Unusable
Incoherent, does not form real Kurdish sentences, or fails to
address the prompt at all.

## Scoring Dimensions
- **Fluency** — does it read naturally?
- **Grammaticality** — is the grammar correct?
- **Vocabulary purity** — does it avoid unnecessary Arabic/Turkish/
  English substitutions when a Kurdish word exists?
- **Relevance** — does it actually answer the prompt?
